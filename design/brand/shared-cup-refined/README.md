# The Shared Cup

The original coffee-and-chessboard symbol, refined for Qehwa Chess V2.

The canonical color SVG is `public/qehwa-chess.svg`. All PNGs are rendered from that vector; do not trace a raster export back into a new logo.

- Canvas: 512 × 512; board inset: 16; corner radius: 32.
- Board: four 240 × 240 squares; cup center: (256, 256).
- Cup: jade outer radius 140, ivory rim radius 136, charcoal coffee radius 108.
- Handle: 104 × 64 rounded rectangle, rotated 45°; opening: 54 × 24. The opening is aligned with the lower-right board square.
- Keep at least one quarter of the mark's width as clear space. Use the icon at 24 px or more; inspect the supplied 32 px favicon for smaller browser uses.
- Colors: jade #1C6749, ivory #FFFDF8, charcoal #212322. The monochrome export uses charcoal and white.
- Keep the proportions, rim and handle intact; avoid shadows, gradients, rotation, stretching or additional outlines.

Public downloads and the construction diagram are available at `/brand/`. Outfit and Tajawal retain their upstream OFL licenses. The cup image in `design/brand/1.jpg` is the original reference.
