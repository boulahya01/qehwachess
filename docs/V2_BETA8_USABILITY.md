# Beta.8 usability acceptance

Status: awaiting real participant sessions. Automated checks and implementation reviews do not count as user acceptance.

Use the isolated test preview. Recruit at least two players and one organizer. Ask each to complete tasks on mobile and desktop, starting from the homepage. Use their normal language; include Arabic and French participants where possible. A platform administrator completes the separate staff task with an existing staff account.

## Facilitator setup

Prepare a test tournament with Discord announcements disabled, an owned club, a membership request and a team. Do not use real events or send announcements during usability sessions. Participants sign into their own accounts themselves. Keep a participant code rather than their email in the record. Obtain permission before recording screens or voices. Do not record credentials or security screens.

Read the task, then stay silent. Do not name navigation destinations, demonstrate the solution or explain roles. Ask the participant to think aloud. Record the first route they choose, pauses, backtracking, errors and the final outcome. If you help, mark the task as assisted.

## Player tasks

1. Find a casual game you could join. Open its details or conversation, then explain the next action. Joining a real event is optional and requires the participant's choice.
2. Find your next joined game and a club you belong to. Explain the difference between an active membership and a pending request.
3. Find the events you organize, then return to your games using the same account. If organizing is not enabled, explain the screen without activating it during this task.
4. Change your display language, locate sign-in settings, then open your public profile. Explain what other people can see. Do not change credentials.

## Organizer tasks

1. Create a draft tournament, leave and return, then explain whether it is saved and visible to other players.
2. Submit the prepared draft for review. Explain who reviews it and what happens after approval. Keep Discord announcements disabled on the fixture.
3. Open your club's events, members, teams and Discord settings. At each destination, identify which club you are managing. Do not send a Discord test.
4. Find how to review a membership request and assign an active member to a team. Use disposable fixtures if executing those actions.

## Administrator task

Find all pending tournaments, review a fixture's details, then explain the effects of approval. Only publish the disposable fixture with announcements disabled. Find statistics and official Discord controls afterwards.

Use a pause of five seconds or an explicit statement of uncertainty as a hesitation. Record backtracks as returning to a previous page to find a different path. Capture the participant's words when they misunderstand a label. Do not replace missing observations with assumptions.

After each task ask: “How easy or difficult was that?” on a 1–7 scale. Ask what they expected before revealing the intended path.

## Acceptance gate

Every critical account and organizer task must be completed independently on both device classes. Any permission confusion, wrong-club action, lost draft, accidental publication, inaccessible primary action or exposed private field blocks acceptance. Refine recurring navigation hesitations, then repeat the affected tasks with another participant. Record the participant count, failures and remaining issues before calling the sprint accepted. A beta preview can be published while this gate remains pending; stable V2 cannot.
