Using the mark

Leave clear space around the mark of at least one quarter of its width. Use the full icon at 24 pixels or larger; check the supplied favicon at smaller sizes. Keep its proportions, use a calm plain background, and pair the icon with the Qehwa Chess name when space allows. Do not stretch the logo, add effects, or use it to imply endorsement.
